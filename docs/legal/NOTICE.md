## School services

IsctCompanion is not an official product of Institute of Science Tokyo and is
not endorsed by it. It connects to university systems (such as the portal, LMS
and course registration) using your own account. You are responsible for using
those systems in line with the university's rules. The university may change
or block access at any time, and features that depend on it may stop working.

## Your data

The program stores your data on your device. Sign-in credentials are kept in
your operating system's credential store. If you turn on cloud backup, a copy
is saved in your own cloud account (for example, Google Drive or iCloud). The
program downloads public reference data, such as academic calendars, from our
data repository on GitHub, which may log connection metadata such as your IP
address. School requests go to the school services. This release includes no
usage analytics and no developer-operated server. Keep backups of important
records and confirm dates, especially exams, in official sources.
