# IsctCompanion privacy policy

Updated: 25 October 2026

IsctCompanion is an independent study companion for Institute of Science Tokyo.
It is developed by Gliese129 and OHNope and is not an official university app.
This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app, including its share extensions.

## Data on your device

The app stores your settings, study plans, school records, downloaded materials,
assignment files and diagnostic logs on your device. School sign-in details and
authentication secrets are stored in the operating system's Keychain. Eligible
Keychain items can synchronize through your iCloud Keychain settings.

If you use Matrix card scanning or import an authentication image, recognition
runs on your device. Camera and photo access are used for the features you
choose. Notifications use the device's notification system.

## School services

When you connect a university account, your device communicates directly with
university services to sign in and retrieve your school information. Files you
choose to send to an LMS assignment draft are transmitted to the university's
LMS. The university controls these accounts and services and applies its own
privacy policies.

The developers do not operate an application server, receive your school
password through a developer service, or keep a server-side copy of your school
records. The app has no advertising or usage analytics SDK.

## Optional cloud synchronization

You can enable iCloud, Google Drive or both to keep copies of workspace data in
your own cloud account and synchronize your devices. Google Drive authorization
requests access to this app's private application-data folder (`drive.appdata`),
not general access to all files in your Drive. The app uses that access to read,
write and manage its backups. It does not use Google account data for advertising,
sell it, or transfer it to a developer-operated server.

Apple and Google process the cloud data and connection information needed to
provide their services under their own privacy policies. Cloud synchronization
is optional. Turning it off does not itself delete existing cloud copies.

## Other network requests and sharing

The app downloads public reference data, such as academic calendars, from the
project's GitHub data repository. Network services, including GitHub and the
university, can receive connection information such as your IP address when your
device contacts them. Links and school web pages you open may connect to other
services under those services' privacy policies.

Backup files and application logs are shared only when you choose an export or
sharing action. Review files before sharing them. Contacting the developers
through GitHub also sends the information you choose to include to GitHub; public
issues are visible to others.

## Your controls and deletion

Settings lets you remove saved school sign-in details, disconnect cloud
synchronization, clear school caches or reset this device. The reset screen lists
what the selected action deletes and keeps. Exported files, course notes,
diagnostic logs, cloud copies and copies on other devices may remain depending
on the action; manage those copies separately in Files, the cloud provider or
the relevant device. System backups and iCloud Keychain are managed in your
Apple account and device settings.

## Contact and changes

Privacy questions can be raised at
[the project issue tracker](https://github.com/isct-companion/isct-companion/issues).
Do not post passwords, authentication tokens or private school records.
Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date.
