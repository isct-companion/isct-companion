- After clearing local data, an open course page now waits for the enrolment list and fills in, instead of staying empty for several minutes.
- Right-click a class on the calendar to mark it as an exam or a quiz. Marked sessions appear in the Exams list, on the course page, in Today's upcoming exams and in exam reminders. A notification setting decides whether quizzes count as exams.
- Exams can be deleted from the exam editor, with Undo in the notice. Deleting a marked session removes only the mark and keeps the class; deleting a published exam takes it off your schedule, and search still finds it. This replaces "Hide from my schedule".
- Your corrections to a published exam's date, time or room now survive timetable updates. If the published timetable changes afterwards, the exam shows both versions and lets you keep yours or use the published one; an exam that leaves the timetable stays visible as removed.
- Exams and quizzes stand out more clearly on the calendar.
- Switching from a course to Exams in Lectures no longer leaves the course's lesson tip on screen.
- Marking sessions and other edits respond faster, and school reads that find nothing new no longer refresh every view.
- Undo and Retry in notices now appear as buttons beside the message.
