- On Windows, campus print jobs no longer fail in the print spooler.
- Signing in no longer loops: a sign-in that changed nothing does not start another one, and an account whose password is gone stops trying.
- When forgetting your school accounts could not finish, Settings now says so with a "Forget again" button, instead of a green badge and school pages failing in English.
- On the desktop, choosing a file for a past exam upload works again, and a PDF preview still shows after you switch pages and come back.
- Calendar-marked exams save in earlier quarters too and keep their mark when edited.
- Past exams from ScienceTokyoExam on the desktop, iPhone, iPad and Android: browse this quarter's courses, open and save papers, upload your own (a single photo becomes a one-page PDF), and ask for answers.
- Classes and Assignments widgets for macOS, iPhone, iPad and Android.
- The campus VPN on the desktop connects by itself when you open a campus address or a library journal, and disconnects when it goes unused. Papers the library subscribes to open off campus. On macOS the helper is allowed once in System Settings › Login Items, with Touch ID, and updates without asking for your password.
- School OTP setup is redesigned into "Help me set up" and "Import existing setup", and shows your live verification code on every platform. The Matrix card can be pasted as text, including titech-app's export.
- LMS forums, pages, surveys and other items open as their LMS page instead of failing. Course ZIP files open as folders, and a PDF with an open password asks for it.
- On exam days, the exam timetable replaces that course's weekly class, and the timetable shows when each period ends.
- School pages send Google sign-in and Gmail to your browser, show the site's sign-in dialog when it asks for one, and open ⌘- or middle-clicked links in your browser.
- The desktop has a new look: one plane under a unified title bar, with the same toolbar style in every pane, and the lessons as a column of ticks beside the course outline.
- On the desktop, first-run tips point at the control they explain and stay inside the window, and the theme swatches show the dark colours in dark mode.
- On iPhone, the Web tab's top and tab bars keep their background over the page and no longer hide while you scroll.
- On Android, a campus map like the one on iPhone. On both phones, the map has a search bar at the top, a locate button, and lets you photograph a building's floors to help complete the map.
- On iPad and Android tablets, all six tabs show at once. On the phones, swiping the timetable to the next week no longer opens the class under your finger.
- Course and syllabus language is chosen by one rule on every platform, and Settings has a feedback form.
