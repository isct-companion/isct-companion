- School sign-in recovers after a failed attempt. Save and connect in Settings now signs in straight away on desktop, iPhone, iPad and Android, and automatic sign-in no longer stays off until the app restarts.
- Settings says why a school sign-in failed: the ID or password, the one-time password, the Matrix answer, or a notice on the school page. A wrong password or Matrix is never retried on its own; a turned-down one-time password is tried again a few times once a new code is live.
- New sign-in details reach your other devices through iCloud Keychain and credential sync only after the school accepts them, so a typo on one device no longer breaks sign-in on the others.
- A password or one-time password saved on another device is used at the next sign-in check, without restarting the app.
