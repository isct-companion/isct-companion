- The Mac app now requires macOS 15 or later. If your Mac runs macOS 14, stay on 1.2.7 and do not install this update, because 1.3.0 will not open there.
- Your data now lives in one database that stays readable while a sync writes, so pages no longer stall during a sync or fail with "database is locked". The first launch of 1.3.0 moves your data over and downloads school data again. Going back to an earlier version afterwards is not supported.
- School sign-in is sturdier. The one-time code is sent once per attempt, and school maintenance pauses that site with its notice instead of counting as a failed password, on sign-in and on LMS and 教務Web reads alike. Connect replaces a running sign-in without sending the password twice, Retry reads your saved password again and says why when it cannot, and the account details tell you when a refused password could not be saved.
- A school page opens first and signs in only if it lands on the sign-in page. Go always takes you to the address, even the one already showing.
- If one school site cannot be reached, reads that do not need it carry on.
- On macOS and Windows, Campus VPN signs in and connects in one press and installs only the campus routes. It can keep connecting for a time you choose, survives a change of network, and Connect can retry after a failure.
- On the desktop, a new Tools page holds Campus VPN, Wi-Fi and the campus printer, which prints under your student ID. About is now a page of its own with a new design.
- Handing in an LMS assignment no longer gives up after three minutes: an unclear result is checked again until it is settled, and success means the files on the LMS match yours.
- Command keys work in school pages on the desktop, campus hosts that only have IPv6 can be reached, and the portal row for Tokyo is shown. On Windows, a long school session is saved correctly.
- The school sign-in field is labelled ID rather than student ID, and local AI agents are told why LMS submissions could not open.
