- School pages sign you in only when you ask: opening a page from the app or pressing Reload on a school sign-in page. While the app signs in, the page is covered with a Cancel, I'll sign in myself button, and your password is sent at most once per attempt.
- On macOS and iOS, Reload on a school page reopens the last page you visited, and tells you when it cannot confirm whether a form you sent went through. On Windows, Linux and Android, Reload reloads the page you are on and cannot warn you about a form you sent.
- After a restart, saved school sign-ins are checked once before they are used, and a network outage or school maintenance no longer leaves a page signed in with an outdated session.
- Signing out of one school account closes its pages and clears only that account's sign-in, leaving the other account signed in. On Android, which cannot clear one site at a time, you can clear every school site's sign-in yourself.
- On macOS, LMS attachments open in place only when they are PDFs, images, audio or video. Other files, such as notebooks or HTML files, are saved to Downloads instead of showing up as garbled text.
